# apriltag

Print files for the RQ2 markers: 25 AprilTag 36h11 boards (IDs 0 to 24), black square 220 mm on a 275 x 275 mm board, prepared for PCV print with 2 mm bleed; one `_CMYK.pdf` (artwork) and one `_CUT.pdf` (cut contour and four 6 mm holes on the layers `cut` and `otwory`) per board. Layout in the field: five rows of five every 5 m, 0.75 m between centres across the track, ID = 5 x row + position, position 0 on the left looking from A to B, the label towards B. Test prints: ID 10 to 12 with a 220 mm square on A3 sheets and with a 140 mm square on A4 for bench tests, and the 9 x 6 chessboard (25 mm squares) used for the camera calibration.
