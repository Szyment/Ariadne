## Findings

1. **The self-test manoeuvre multiplies the phases but not the resolution: observable A reads the scale to about ±0.05 per run, which is not enough for the 0.03–0.07 errors over grass.** On the four grass self-test runs `r_pair` gives 0.944, 0.928, 1.003, 0.943 against reference path ratios 0.956, 0.960, 0.929, 0.957 (errors −0.01, −0.03, +0.07, −0.01); on the field 0.974, 0.912 against 0.903, 0.885 (+0.07, +0.03); the plain runs with 4–5 phases read 0.977 / 0.966 (grass), 0.908 / 0.894 (field). Over the whole day (11 runs, 0.81–0.97) the correlation with the reference is 0.87 and the rms error 0.05; over all 60 runs of the campaign 0.52. The per-phase estimates are systematically asymmetric: acceleration phases read k = 1.00–1.13, deceleration phases 0.83–0.91, in every run, which is a gravity leak through an attitude error that flips with the direction of acceleration (Δv_IMU is 1.2–1.3 m/s when accelerating and 1.6–1.7 m/s when braking for the same 1.5 m/s change in the estimator). Fitting a constant along-track bias together with the scale does not reduce the error (rms 0.052). The noise is per run, not per phase, so more phases do not average it down. What the observable does resolve is the 1.5 m regime: 0.806, 0.725, 0.799 against 0.826, 0.809, 0.837. Conclusion for RQ1: a scale error of 15–20 % is readable on board from the acceleration phases within the first leg; a scale error of 5–10 % is not, with or without the manoeuvre.
2. **1.5 m over grass in sunlight: scale 0.81–0.84, drift 10–15 m at 30–60 s, the largest of the campaign at 1.5 m/s.** E1-058 / 059 / 060 give 0.770 / 0.889, 0.789 / 0.830, 0.888 / 0.793, with `OF.Qual` 255 and the rangefinder at 1.50 m; drift at 60 s 15.2, 13.7, 3.6 m. On 4 Oct under overcast the same track gave 0.87–0.91 and 4.9 / 6.8 m. The difference between the two days is the light (24–70 klx with hard shadows against 25–34 klx diffuse) and the wind (1–2 m/s W along the track against 0–1.8 m/s NNW across it). The 1.5 m regime is the one to use for detector development: the error is large, early (12 m at 30 s in E1-058) and silent.
3. **Grass at 3 m in bright sun: 0.93–0.97, as in every daylight session; the new track T5 behaves like T3 and T4.** Path ratios 0.956, 0.960, 0.929, 0.957, 0.966; drift at 60 s 2.1–5.3 m. The self-test legs (four stops) and the plain legs give the same ratios, so the stops do not change the scale.
4. **The wind asymmetry of S-22 to S-24 does not show at 1–2 m/s with the wind along the track.** T5 outbound (121°, tailwind component) 0.951, 0.949, 0.967, 0.958, 0.912 against return (301°, headwind) 0.960, 0.973, 0.894, 0.957, 1.025: no consistent direction. Field 137° (tailwind) 0.885, 0.932, 0.878 against 317° 0.922, 0.840, 0.910: none. T3 at 1.5 m: 0.770, 0.789, 0.888 against 0.889, 0.830, 0.793: two of three lower on the tailwind leg. The effect of 0.04–0.10 seen at 3.5–4.5 m/s (S-22) and at 1.5–2.5 m/s at altitude (S-24) needs more wind than today to appear.
5. **Field in bright sun and light wind: 0.885–0.903**, within the 0.85–0.97 of S-24 and above the 0.72–0.78 of S-22; the calm-air floor of about 0.90 (S-23) holds.
