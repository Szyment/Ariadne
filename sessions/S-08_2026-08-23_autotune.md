## Rate of illuminance decline at dusk

Four readings within 38 minutes give a decline from 1091 to 69 lx, i.e. **15.8×**.

| Interval | Time | Decline | Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1091 → 228.5 lx | 18 min | −79.1% | −8.69 %/min |
| 228.5 → 118.2 lx | 11 min | −48.3% | −5.99 %/min |
| 118.2 → 69 lx | 9 min | −41.6% | −5.98 %/min |

Exponential fit for the whole: time constant **13.8 min**, rate **−7.27 %/min**.

Consequences for protocol §8, which rejects a run when illuminance changes by more than 30%:

| | |
|---|---|
| Time to a 30% decline | **4.9 min** |
| Change during a 90 s run | −10.3% |
| Change over a cell of 3 repetitions at about 3 min each | −48% |
| Change over a cell of 5 repetitions at about 3 min each | −66% |

A single run fits within the criterion. **A cell of three repetitions no longer does.** The measurement of 21 Aug gave −64% in 9 minutes and led to the same conclusion; this record sharpens it and gives a number: at dusk, one evening window accommodates **at most two runs of the same cell**.

## Temperature gradient reversal

| | Ground | Air | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23 Aug, 12:54 (S-07) | 30 °C | 22–24 °C | ground warmer by 6–8 K |
| 23 Aug, 19:10 (S-08) | 17.8 °C | 19.2 °C | **air warmer by 1.4 K** |

Within seven hours the sign of the difference reversed. The ground radiated heat away faster than the air, which gives **stable stratification**, and that suppresses turbulence near the ground. The measured wind of 0.0–1.6 m/s is a consequence of this.

**Operational conclusion: the evening after the gradient reversal is the time of weakest wind and the best moment for autotune and for runs requiring calm air.** To be used in planning the campaign sessions.

The grass is cooler than the ground and the difference grows as the evening progresses: 1.6 K → 2.1 K → 2.3 K. Vegetation radiates heat away faster than bare soil.

## Repeatability of the procedure

The first run (log 12) ended in success, but the gains were not saved: the pilot switched to AltHold before landing, which restores the original values. The repetition on the second pack gave **two independent tunings of the same platform 25 minutes apart.**

The conditions were not identical: flight 1 took place at 1.1 m/s with gusts to 1.6 m/s, flight 2 in practically calm air. The spread below therefore mixes **the non-repeatability of the procedure itself with the real difference in conditions** and is an upper bound on the former.

| | Log 12 | Log 13 | Spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roll, Rate P | 0.114 | 0.114 | **0.0%** |
| Roll, Rate D | 0.0038 | 0.0033 | 14% |
| Roll, Angle P | 12.238 | 9.589 | **24%** |
| Pitch, Rate P | 0.102 | 0.121 | 17% |
| Pitch, Rate D | 0.0036 | 0.0040 | 11% |
| Pitch, Angle P | 11.073 | 11.100 | **0.2%** |

**The spread ranges from 0 to 24% and has no pattern.** In roll the rate loop repeated and the angle loop diverged; in pitch the reverse. Neither the axis nor the type of loop is systematically repeatable.

Conclusion for the campaign: **the gains are not a reproducible property of the platform, but the result of a single flight with an uncertainty reaching a quarter of the value.** They must remain frozen for the whole campaign, and the date they were established recorded. Re-tuning during the campaign would invalidate the between-session comparisons.

The run that was saved is the one flown in calm conditions, i.e. the more favourable of the two. This happened by chance; the save depended on the mode in which the pilot landed, not on the quality of the conditions.

## Procedural finding

The gains are saved **only on disarming in the tuning mode**. A mode change before landing restores the original values and discards the result, despite the `Success` message.

Cost of the mistake: one pack and one flight. Gain: a measurement of the repeatability of the procedure, which would otherwise not exist.

## Side note from the snapshot comparison

Between the 20:34 and 20:36 snapshots the controller restarted. `INS_GYR1_CALTEMP` rose from **19.3 to 42.7 °C**: `INS_GYR_CAL` = 1 calibrates the gyroscopes at every start-up, and the board heater (`BRD_HEAT_TARG` = 45 °C) had not reached temperature before the first of these starts.

This is a probable explanation of the `Arm: Gyros inconsistent` message noted in card S-06: calibration on a cold board. To be checked whether the message appears only when arming earlier than a few minutes after power-on.
